About

Hello!

My name is Rafa! I grew up in Plymouth, Minnesota, studied computer science (with a minor in cinema and media studies) at the University of Minnesota, and spent a semester abroad in Ireland — which is where the four-leaf clover comes from. These days I call New York City home.

I like tinkering — building things, breaking things, and putting them back together, in code and otherwise. Outside of work you'll find me rock climbing, traveling when I can, and shooting film along the way.

Feel free to send me an email if you want to connect or collaborate on something exciting!
