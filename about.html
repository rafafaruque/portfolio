About

Hi there!

My name is Rafa! I grew up in Plymouth, Minnesota and studied computer science with a minor in cinema and media studies at the University of Minnesota (ski u mah!). Somewhere in there I took a detour to Ireland for a semester — hence the four-leaf clover — and picked up a lifelong habit of saying yes to plane tickets. Last summer I packed up for New York City and haven't slowed down since.

I'm a serial tinkerer — building things, breaking things, and (usually) putting them back together, in code and in life. Outside of work you'll find me climbing or free-running across whatever random surface looks interesting, traveling whenever I can, and shooting film along the way.

Feel free to send me an email if you want to connect or collaborate on something exciting!
